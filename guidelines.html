Honor Code of Players

  • All participants must act in a sportsmanlike manner and respect authorities, referees, family advisers, and published rules.
  • Participants are expected to be punctual, disciplined, and committed to their registered games and events.
  • Disagreements must never be resolved through violence, threats, or similar conduct.
  • Violations may result in suspension from succeeding games, subject to investigation and hearing.

Official Family Composition

The five official families are Huru Bakunawa, Gatdulang Tala, Lakambakod, Sinaghuna, and Adlaw Lawin. Family 1 has 72 members, Family 2 has 70 members, and the other families have 71 members each, formed through a randomizer. No other teams may be included.

Eligibility & Default

  • Only bona fide SACCI students may participate.
  • Every player must submit a photocopy of their School ID and be included in the official roster.
  • Sports and Cheerdance participants must also submit a medical certificate confirming fitness to play.
  • A listed player who remains absent 15 minutes after the official start may be declared in default.
  • Eligibility or Honor Code violations may lead to disqualification or suspension, subject to due process.

Grievance Procedure

  1. Report the concern first to the family adviser and complete the official SSC Grievance Form.
  2. File the complaint within 24 hours of the incident. The SSC reviews and resolves it at their level within 12 hours.
  3. An SSC resolution may be appealed to the Sports Council within 24 hours of receipt; the Council resolves within 24 hours.
  4. A Sports Council resolution, or a serious-misconduct issue, may be elevated to the School Administrators within 12 hours. Their decision is final and binding.
  5. All forms, actions, and resolutions must be documented and signed.
